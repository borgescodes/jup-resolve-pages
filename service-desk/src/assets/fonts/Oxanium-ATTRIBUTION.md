Official Google Fonts source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/oxanium

OxaniumVariable.woff2 is a lossless WOFF2 conversion of that complete variable font (weights 200–800), with Portuguese glyph coverage retained. SVG display outlines are freshly shaped with HarfBuzz and drawn from the 800-weight Oxanium instance; no outlines from the previous display font are reused. Conversion tools: fontTools 4.66.1, Brotli 1.2.0, uharfbuzz 0.56.3. The font's family name and original copyright records are preserved.
